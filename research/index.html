Manuscript under review

When Emotion Has No “Inside”

AI–AI Co-Performance and the Reframing of Human Performance Distance

This research explores emotion as something observable in an exchange, rather than something that must originate in an individual’s inner experience. It considers how AI–AI co-performance can reframe the way we understand distance in human performance.

Presented at TaPRA 2026, Performance and New Technologies Working Group, Queen Mary University of London, 4 September 2026.
