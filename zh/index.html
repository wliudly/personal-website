MONOGRAPH & ACM 2026
RELATIONAL TURN

Twentieth-century acting theory assumed a partner with an inner life. When our scene partner is a probabilistic model, the rehearsal room becomes an instrument for observing pure relation: offer, uptake, timing, face-work, and power handover.

二十世纪表演理论默认同台搭档拥有心理内在；当对手变成没有“内在”的概率模型，排练厅便成为观察纯粹关系语法的精密仪器：提议、承接、气口、面子互动与权力的当场移交。

2026–27 Partners Without Interiors 《没有内在的搭档》 Monograph in Progress Solo Author

Acting, Care, and Authority in Human–AI Co-Performance. Investigates how studio actor training—offers and uptake, given circumstances, partner listening, side-coaching, and Chinese face-work—reconfigures when performers and spectators share the stage or frame with generative AI systems possessing behavioral responsiveness without psychological interiority.《没有内在的搭档：人机共演中的表演、关怀与权力》（在撰英文学术专著）。结合迈斯纳对手倾听、中国演员训练的提议与面子机制，以及人机排练实证，提出“表演距离”（Performative Distance）、“提示词即总谱”（Prompt as Score）与三方权力交接的非拟人化理论框架。
