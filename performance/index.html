Character & improvisation

Finding the character through action.

In a 2017 interview about Nu Guanjia, I described finding Jiabao He through close reading of the script and playful physical choices. During one scene, the image of a dog seeking reassurance became an improvised gesture—a way into the character’s dependence and vulnerability. The moment was not included in the final edit, but it helped me understand him.

Young Marshal: a character built from history

Preparing Chengye Xu meant researching several of Zhang Xueliang’s historical adjutants. Their stories offered a basis for a fictional character whose relationship with Zhang combines professional duty, friendship and loyalty.
