Improvisation Studio

即兴片场

Improvisation Studio: An Audience-Directed Laboratory of Human–AI Co-Performance

Year
2026
Form
Interactive installation
Exhibition
ICHEC 2026 Art Gallery, Wynn Macau, Macao SAR
Dates
24–25 November 2026

What happens when a visitor takes the place of an acting teacher? Improvisation Studio is an interactive installation in which a visitor sets an improvisation exercise for two AI performers and then decides how far to enter it. A language model generates the given circumstances and each character’s objective, then produces every line live; nothing is pre-scripted. At any moment the visitor can pause the scene, give a private direction to one character, take over a role, or walk on as a third character whose lines only a human can speak.

The work turns established rehearsal-room practices, such as offers, uptake, objectives and side-coaching, into an interface through which the public can feel how authority and responsibility are redistributed when people and machines perform together.
