Dad Is Not Home

老爸唔在家

Form
Original improvised play, about 70 minutes, in Mandarin
Role
Dramaturg, director, playwright (the acts “Ten Years Later” and “Twenty Years Later”) and supervising teacher
Performance
13 November 2025, 19:30, Dhall, Macau University of Science and Technology
Context
End-of-term presentation of the Class of 2022 Stage Performance III course
Status
Selected in the initial round of the inaugural Greater Bay Area Campus Theatre Season (GYTF 2026; announced 21 August 2026) and advanced to the second review

An original improvised play (approx. 70 minutes, in Mandarin), created as the end-of-term presentation of the Class of 2022 Stage Performance III course. Performed on 13 November 2025, 19:30, Dhall, Macau University of Science and Technology. Selected in the initial round of the inaugural Greater Bay Area Campus Theatre Season (GYTF 2026; announced 21 August 2026) and advanced to the second review.
