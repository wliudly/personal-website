Workshops

Workshops in Improvisation & Applied Theatre — in English and Mandarin

I lead bilingual (English / Mandarin) workshops that use improvisation to build listening, trust and shared storytelling, drawing on actor training (including the Meisner technique) and my research on human–AI co-performance:

“Yes, and.”

Accept an offer, add something specific, build the scene together.

Attention

Listen to a partner, notice a change, respond to what is actually happening.

Reflection

Step out of the exercise to talk about how choices shape relationships.

Suitable for community groups, artists and students, with or without performance experience.

7–10 May 2026 · Macao

From Breath to Expression: Community Public Performance Workshop

「从呼吸到表达」社区公众表演体验工作坊

Curated and led by Wei Liu, with guest instructor 高詠詩. Four days, about three hours a day, free and open to the public; 21 participants aged 18–50. Breathing, Michael Chekhov technique, Alexander Technique and improvisation. Funded by the Macao Foundation; organised by the Macau University of Science and Technology Foundation, with the Academy of Film as co-organiser. Venue: PP-R215.
